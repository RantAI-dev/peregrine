Part 2: the exact wide pass. One real layer (2048 x 8192, 1024 eight-row tiles, 9.4 MB
streamed), one thread pinned to CPU 4. `KQBENCH_LAYER=1 KQBENCH_WIDE=1 kqbench 8 40 1`.
MACs per call: 8192 rows x 2048 weights x nb activations. "today" is q4k8_gemm_zmm,
"widened" is q4k8_gemm_zmm_wide (the tile's nibbles copied to one byte per weight once per
tile, then the same kernel), and every width is asserted bit-identical to "today" before it is
timed. "widening alone" is q4k8_widen by itself, expressed as the weight-MAC rate the same work
would be (it computes no products; the figure is the pass it costs).

Conclusion: the widening is exact (asserted at every width, and by
the_widened_wide_pass_is_bit_identical_to_the_narrow_one from nb 4 to 40) and worth nothing at
any width a wide pass would use. The nibble split is free; what costs is the per-32 scale
multiply and the min term, which exactness forbids folding into the code.
